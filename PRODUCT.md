# Ewear

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Angular with TypeScript frontend and ASP.NET Core C# backend, specified by the user.

## Users

Shoppers browse shirts, pants, and boots, add items to a cart, and access an account through phone verification. Company employees manage the catalog through a protected staff account.

## Product Purpose

An online clothing storefront with a smooth, modern shopping experience and a manageable product catalog.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Browse products by category and add them to a cart.
- Register and log in with phone number verification.
- Restrict product creation and updates to authorized employees.
- Primary country, SMS provider credentials, payment and fulfillment requirements remain open decisions.

## Brand Commitments

The store name is Ewear. The confirmed palette is #FFD3AC, #FFB5AB, #E39A7B, and #DBB06B. No logo or existing product assets were supplied.

## Evidence on Hand

No existing product photos, copy, pricing, or customer evidence were supplied. Seed catalog content is illustrative.

## Product Principles

- Make product discovery and cart changes immediate and clear.
- Confirm bag additions without interrupting product browsing; open the bag only when requested.
- Preserve a clear, low-contrast dark theme alongside the four-color brand palette.
- Keep account access simple while protecting staff operations.
- Keep configurable services such as SMS outside the source code.
